Good questions. Clear answers.

Start here for the practical details of Reclaim Yourself. If your question is more personal, speak to the team.

Is this retreat for women only?
No. Reclaim Yourself is a mixed-gender retreat. The group is limited to 15 participants.
Can I come on my own?
Yes. You can request a shared room or ask for a private-room quote. Room arrangements and any matching preferences are confirmed by the team before booking.
Is this therapy?
This is a travel and wellbeing retreat with guided reflection. It is not clinical treatment or a substitute for individual mental-health care. Speak with the team about whether the format suits you.
Do I need experience with movement or reflection?
You do not need to arrive with a polished practice. Ask about the activity level, access needs and adaptations before booking; the team will confirm what can be supported.
Are flights, visas and insurance included?
Flights and visas are not included. Travel insurance is included; the insurer, policy coverage, eligibility and exclusions must be confirmed in your final booking documents.
Which meals are included?
The supplied itinerary includes daily breakfast and dinner, plus lunch on the Phi Phi day. Coverage for other lunches needs final confirmation before bookings open.
Can I stay for an extra night?
Yes, you can request an extra night. Availability, room category and the final rate are confirmed separately. It is not automatically added to your booking.
What happens if the itinerary changes?
Sea conditions, weather and local operations can affect excursions. The team will communicate the confirmed schedule and any necessary changes. Final change and cancellation terms must be approved before sale.
Can I pay a deposit?
A deposit arrangement is under review. The standard website flow is full payment. If a deposit option is approved, the team will issue a quotation with the exact amount, balance date and terms.
How do I speak to someone?
WhatsApp will be the main contact, with email as a fallback. Contact details are awaiting confirmation in this preview. Use the contact page to see the planned enquiry experience.
