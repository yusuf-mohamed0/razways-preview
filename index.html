Seven days.
Your own unfolding.

Open each day for a closer look. There is a rhythm to the week, with room to breathe between the moments.

Proposed programme. Timing, excursion operators and weather-dependent activities need final confirmation.

16JanuaryArrive, exhale, settle in

Airport arrival and transfer to Island Escape by Burasari. Meet your hosts and the group at the 4:30 pm welcome circle, followed by dinner.

Day 1 · Arrival and welcome

17JanuaryMeet a different side of Phuket

Begin with movement and reflection. Discover Old Phuket Town and its market, with ATV and zipline activities in the proposed programme. Participation requirements and alternatives will be confirmed.

Day 2 · Movement and exploration

18JanuaryA day shaped by the sea

Sail towards Racha and Coral Islands, enjoy time on the water and return with the sunset. An opening-heart circle creates space to reflect together.

Day 3 · Sailing and connection

19JanuaryMake room for wonder

Gentle movement, a surprise stop and a proposed elephant-sanctuary visit. Close the day with storytelling. Operator and animal-welfare checks are required before this excursion is confirmed.

Day 4 · Discovery and storytelling

20JanuaryBeyond the familiar shore

An early start for the Phi Phi and Maya Bay boat day. Lunch is included on this excursion. Sea conditions and local access rules may change the route.

Day 5 · Island exploration

21JanuaryFollow your own rhythm

Free time from 9 am to 6 pm: rest, journal or choose your own plans. Optional personal activities are separate. Gather at 6 pm for the farewell dinner.

Day 6 · Space and celebration

22JanuaryCarry something home

Movement and a final sharing circle, then departure and airport transfers. Exact timing will be coordinated with the group’s flight arrangements.

Day 7 · Closing and departure

A few things you may be wondering.

Read all questions
Is this retreat for women only?
No. Reclaim Yourself is a mixed-gender retreat. The group is limited to 15 participants.
Can I come on my own?
Yes. You can request a shared room or ask for a private-room quote. Room arrangements and any matching preferences are confirmed by the team before booking.
Is this therapy?
This is a travel and wellbeing retreat with guided reflection. It is not clinical treatment or a substitute for individual mental-health care. Speak with the team about whether the format suits you.
Do I need experience with movement or reflection?
You do not need to arrive with a polished practice. Ask about the activity level, access needs and adaptations before booking; the team will confirm what can be supported.
Are flights, visas and insurance included?
Flights and visas are not included. Travel insurance is included; the insurer, policy coverage, eligibility and exclusions must be confirmed in your final booking documents.
