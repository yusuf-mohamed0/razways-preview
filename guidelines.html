Sharing the journey.

A proposed set of community expectations for a small, mixed-gender retreat.

Draft for review. This is a designed policy structure, not approved legal or booking terms.

Respect different paths

Listen without pressure to agree. People may choose how much they share; participation in personal reflection should not require disclosure.

Consent comes first

Ask before taking or posting another participant’s photograph. Respect personal space, boundaries and a clear no.

Keep personal stories private

Do not repeat someone else’s personal experience outside the group without their permission. The final participation agreement should explain any safety-related limits.

Care for the place

Follow local guidance, respect wildlife and communities, and leave shared spaces thoughtfully.

Speak to your hosts

Raise discomfort, access needs or practical concerns early. The live version must include host contacts, emergency arrangements and an agreed response process.
