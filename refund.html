Cancellation & refunds

Clear terms are part of a considered journey.

Draft for review. This is a designed policy structure, not approved legal or booking terms.

Policy status

Cancellation deadlines, refund amounts, transfer rights and organiser-cancellation terms have not been approved. No refund percentage or non-refundable deposit is promised in this preview.

Before accepting a booking

Publish the cancellation timeline with exact dates, the amounts retained or refunded, processing method, currency and the handling of provider charges where applicable.

If the organiser changes or cancels the trip

The approved policy must state the guest’s options, communication process, refund timeline and treatment of independently booked travel.

How to request a cancellation

The final policy will name the official WhatsApp and email contacts. Guests should include the booking reference and participant name; identity checks may be needed.

Do not open sales yet

Link the approved policy from the event page, checkout and confirmation email. Archive the version accepted with each booking.
