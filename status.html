On the live site, your confirmation email and Odoo customer portal will show the current status of your order.

Design reference: no live order lookup is connected.

What happens next?

Payment confirmed
You receive a confirmed order reference, participant information and the next steps for preparing for the retreat.
Payment pending
Wait for the provider and Odoo to update the order. Check the existing order before attempting another payment. Contact RazWays with the reference if you need help.
Payment failed or cancelled
Return to the same order to review available payment methods, or contact the team. A failed payment is not a confirmed reservation.
A quotation is awaiting approval
Review the room, participants, payment terms, balance date and cancellation policy before accepting. A quotation is not itself a guarantee of a seat.
