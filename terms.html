Booking terms

Understand your booking before you commit.

Draft for review. This is a designed policy structure, not approved legal or booking terms.

The retreat

The planned retreat takes place in Phuket, Thailand, from 16–22 January 2027, for six nights and seven days. It is a mixed-gender group with a planned capacity of 15 participants.

What your quote must specify

Participant names, room category, accommodation basis, inclusions and exclusions, total price and currency, taxes, payment schedule, confirmation conditions and cancellation terms.

Travel and participation

Guests arrange their own flights and visas. Share access and activity-related requirements privately with the team before booking. Confirm activity eligibility and the included insurance policy’s terms.

Programme changes

Weather, sea conditions and local operations can affect activities. The final agreement must explain substitutions, material changes and the options available to guests.

Required before publication

Legal entity and address, governing law, complaint channel, minimum age, liability provisions, insurance details, tax treatment and approval of all booking conditions.
